GIRLS IN LUXEMBOURG

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers before your first hello. Find answers about joining, activities and life in the community.

Joining & Membership

Is it free to join?

You don’t need a paid membership to join our community! Most of our events are free to attend, while some special events are paid. Every woman is welcome to be part of our Girls in Lux WhatsApp Community. We’re all about creating connections, sharing experiences, and building a supportive space where everyone feels welcome!

What language do I need to speak?

English is our main language for online communication and community posts, but we’re a multilingual community!

Our community leads speak Luxembourgish, French, English, and Spanish, so feel free to communicate in whichever of these languages you feel most comfortable with.

If you’re interested in learning or teaching a language, we also encourage you to join our Language Exchange Group!

Can I join more than one sub-group?

Of course! Join as many as genuinely interest you.

How do I actually get into a private group chat?

Use our WhatsApp invitation link to join the community. WhatsApp will guide you through the next steps. You can then ask the community admins about the groups you would like to join.

Is this only for women? Can men come to any events?

Girls in Luxembourg is a women’s community at its core, so our groups and events are women-only.

Do I need to already live in Luxembourg or border regions to join?

Yes, to ensure the purpose of this community, we only accept women who live or work in Luxembourg and border regions of France, Germany and Belgium.

WhatsApp groups & Events

How do I find out what events are happening?

We communicate our events in our socials (Instagram, TikTok) and in the WhatsApp group dedicated to the topic of the event. You can also see them all on our events calendar.

Can I suggest a new event or activity on a WhatsApp group?

You’re welcome to share interesting opportunities and suggestions, but please avoid using the group solely for self-promotion.

To keep the community space welcoming and balanced, you can reach out to us using the contact form on our website before promoting your own services, paid events, or business, or proposing a collaboration. We’re always open to discussing potential partnerships!

Thank you for helping us keep this community a space for genuine connections, sharing, and support!

What if I can’t attend an event?

First of all, please reach out to the organiser as soon as you know that you won’t be able to come, to stay informed. Some events have a certain level of organisation, and this helps us to readjust. Besides, that free slot can be used for someone who could not attend the event because the ticket was sold out, so you are giving this opportunity to them. If the event has a cost, we will follow our refund policy.

Are events free, or do some cost money?

Depending on the event, some events might have a cost. This price covers the logistics of the event. Note that we work to make the events affordable for everyone.

Can I start a new WhatsApp group if it doesn’t exist yet?

For the moment, we are not accepting new proposals. This ensures the well management of the current ones. You can explore the community here.

Community guidelines & safety

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, be respectful, keep shared personal information private, and treat every group with the same care you’d want shown to you. Group leads and admins can remove members who don’t.

All members are kindly asked to read and familiarise themselves with our community guidelines before joining. This helps us maintain a welcoming, respectful, and positive space for all women.

What happens if someone breaks the rules?

The admins will assess the situation and will take the necessary precautions accordingly. The member might be permanently removed from the community.

Is my personal information (like my number or email) shared with other members?

When you join a WhatsApp group, your phone number will be visible for others. The information you share when you use the ticket is used only for organisation purposes of that specific event.

We do not keep this information for other reasons.

Who do I contact if something feels off at an event?

You can reach out to the organiser of the event, send a WhatsApp message to the admin of the group under which the event was created, or share your feedback using our feedback form.

Promotions & self-promotion

Can I promote my own business or service in the groups?

You can give a short presentation the first time you join the WhatsApp community, explaining what you do, but we don’t accept long texts or spam messages. You can reach out to us using the contact form on our website for other types of promotion. Select Partnership and send us your proposal.

Can I share a paid opportunity or job posting?

Yes, under the Career group, with the agreement of the admin (Enare) if it is on WhatsApp or (Marta) if it is on LinkedIn.

Do you ever post sponsored content or brand partnerships?

We currently do not have this content, but you can be the first. For this, please reach out to us using the contact form. Select Partnership and send us your proposal.

Can I ask other members for referrals or recommendations?

Sure! We are here to build any type of connection and support.

Practical / logistics

How do I contact the team directly?

Our team members are available in the WhatsApp groups they manage. You can easily recognise them by the Admin tag next to their names.

Can I leave a WhatsApp group or the community anytime?

Of course, there is no commitment to stay. In case you leave, we will encourage you to share your feedback using this form on our website. We want to understand what works and what doesn’t, so we can implement changes.

I filled out the join form. How long until I hear back?

We want to ensure the correct assessment so it might take 2-4 days, but not longer than one week.

Do you have an age requirement?

We don’t have a strict age requirement for joining our community!

We welcome women of all ages, from 16-year-olds to women aged 40 and above. What matters most is your interest, enthusiasm, and willingness to connect and participate.

For certain events involving drinks, we may ask younger participants to confirm their age to ensure everyone’s safety and comply with age restrictions.

Don’t worry, non-alcoholic drinks are also available as an option!

Refund policy

Most of what we do is free, but some events have a cost to cover logistics (venue, human or material costs). Prices are listed clearly before you sign up.

If you cancel more than 7 days before the event, you’ll get a full refund. If you cancel within 7 days, we can only offer a refund if we’re able to fill your spot from a waitlist. Otherwise, it’s non-refundable, since venue and material costs are usually already committed.

If we cancel or reschedule an event, you’ll always get a full refund or the option to roll your payment over to the new date.

To request a refund, contact us at hello@girlsinlu.com with the event name and date.

Privacy & safe space

This community is intended to be a supportive space for women. Members are free to share any personal experiences, challenges, or difficult life situations within the group chats.

However, stories, personal information, photos or conversations shared in the group should not be discussed elsewhere without the person’s explicit consent.
