GIRLS IN LUXEMBOURG

About us.

This is a community where women come together to build genuine friendships, share experiences and support one another.

Across the Grand Duchy and neighbouring regions of Belgium, Germany and France, we create opportunities for women to connect, discover new experiences, build meaningful relationships and develop a stronger sense of belonging.

It started with
a simple “what if?”

950+ women. So many possibilities.Your next friendship could start here.

Everything started as a simple “what-if” thought in November 2025. Instagram was our first initiative, then WhatsApp. In less than a year, the community grew to hundreds of members all around Luxembourg.

A small country doesn’t have to mean a small circle. That’s exactly why we created Girls in Luxembourg. Meet-ups, sports, hiking, crafts, and many other social activities are a daily part of our community.

Whatever you’re into, there’s already a group of women doing it. Don’t hesitate to join, we would be more than happy to meet you!

01

Luxembourg is not just an address.

Luxembourg is a small country with a lively, multicultural atmosphere. We want to bring that sense of closeness to our community: familiar faces, genuine connections and a feeling of making this country a real home, rather than just an address.

02

Strength through community.

Learning a language, figuring out your career, picking up a new hobby: it’s all easier and more fun with company. In our different groups, you’ll meet women who share your interests and understand your needs.

03

Genuine connections.

Whether you just landed in Luxembourg or you’ve been here your whole life, having people to call when you need a hand, a laugh, or someone to hang out with matters for us.
