YOUR ADMIN SPACE

Hello, and welcome.

This is the back office of Girls in Luxembourg, the place where the community is looked after behind the scenes.

From here you can welcome new members, answer messages, read what the community thinks, keep the figures and photos of the website up to date, and keep the database tidy.

Choose a section in the menu to get started. Everything you save here is updated on the live website.

FROM THE JOIN US FORM

Membership requests

People who asked to join the WhatsApp community. Contact them on WhatsApp, then update their status.

    Community numbers

    Update the four figures displayed on the homepage. Enter numbers only, without commas or +. The + sign and counting animation are kept on the site.

    FROM THE CONTACT PAGE

    Messages

    Messages sent through the contact form. Reply by email, then mark them as replied.

      ANONYMOUS FEEDBACK

      Feedback

      Answers from the feedback form. They are anonymous unless the person chose to leave contact details.

        MAKE IT YOURS

        Website images

        Browse by page and section. Choose an existing photo or upload your own, preview it, then save. Changes are applied to the selected location; shared brand images update across the site.

        ALL YOUR PHOTOS

        Photo library

        Every image stored in the online database, newest first. Tap a photo to see it and where it is used. Use Select to delete several at once; photos shown on the website are protected until you replace them.
