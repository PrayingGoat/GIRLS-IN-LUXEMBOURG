GIRLS IN LUXEMBOURG

Our community.

Shared interests. New friendships. Discover the groups that bring us together.

01EVERYDAY CONNECTIONS

GirlsClub

This is our main community chat: a place where everything comes together! From Beauty & Coffee meetups to everyday conversations, fun outings and cinema nights.

It’s where we share ideas, make plans, discover new experiences, support one another and, most importantly, get to know each other better.

Whether it’s a spontaneous conversation, a girls’ night out, planning our next meetup or simply sharing a little moment from your day, there’s always room to connect.

Led by Fabrina

02READ & SHARE

BookClub

Our refuge for all women who love reading. A cosy and welcoming space to share your favourite reads, discover new books, exchange thoughts, and connect with like-minded women over the magic of storytelling.

Led by Cynthia

03MOTHERHOOD & SUPPORT

Moms'Club

Being a mom is a beautiful journey, and having a supportive community around you can make it even more meaningful. This space welcomes moms and moms-to-be to connect, share experiences, discover new friendships, and enjoy the little moments of motherhood together.

Led by Zahra

04OUTDOORS & ADVENTURE

Walk/HikeClub

An active space for women in Luxembourg who love to explore the city through casual walks or hiking adventures!

Led by Fabrina and Elizabeth

05SPORT & WELLBEING

FitClub

A women's space dedicated to staying active and having fun while doing it! From badminton, paddle and tennis to yoga, pilates and run club, this group brings together all things sport and movement.

Led by Christina

06WORK & OPPORTUNITIES

CareerClub

This space invites you to connect, meet like-minded women, empower your professional skills, network and find your next role of your career.

Led by Marta (LinkedIn group) and Enare (WhatsApp group).

07MAKE & CREATE

Craft Corner

A relaxed space to make things with your hands and pick up a new skill from someone else in the room.

Led by Marta

08LEARN & CONNECT

Language Exchange

Whether you’re looking for one-to-one sessions or interactive group sessions, this space helps you find people to practise or improve your language skills with.

Led by Marta

09DISCOVER & EXPLORE

TravelClub

A space where women come together to share everything about travelling! From hidden gems and city guides to travel tips and country discoveries, this is your go-to room for all things adventure. We share advice, recommendations, and inspire each other to explore the world.

Led by Enare
